Easy to get through, from the first day

Every screen here was designed around one idea: the work should flow, even for someone using Scribient for the very first time. Each screen asks for one thing at a time, says what's needed and why, and fills in what it already knows, so nobody is left guessing.

And nobody stays stuck. Quill helps MAs, the front desk and doctors, and Orbit helps admins. Either one can explain what's on the screen, walk you through it step by step, or take care of the task with you.
